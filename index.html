The Investment Lens

Don't buy the brochure.
Understand the asset.

Before selecting a property, we recommend looking at the location, developer, payment structure, handover timeline, unit economics and the buyer's own investment objective.

Discuss Your Budget

01 — Location

Understand connectivity, surrounding infrastructure, future development and the target tenant/buyer profile.

02 — Developer

Review the developer's official project information, current pipeline and relevant delivery history.

03 — Payment Structure

Compare the actual payment schedule rather than relying only on the headline launch price.

04 — Exit & Rental Logic

Consider rental demand, service charges, financing, holding period and realistic exit scenarios.

Verification First

Luxury is not just appearance.

For off-plan property, always verify the specific project, documentation, payment instructions and escrow information through the appropriate official channels before transferring funds.

Project Verification

Confirm project registration and current status through official Dubai Land Department channels where applicable.

Escrow Awareness

Off-plan projects in Dubai are subject to escrow-account regulations; project-specific details should be checked.

Documentation

Review the official offer, SPA, payment schedule and applicable project documents before committing.
